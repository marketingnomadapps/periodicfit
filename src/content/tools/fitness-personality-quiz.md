## Why your training personality matters

The best program is the one you'll still be doing in six months. Research on exercise adherence keeps pointing to the same thing: **enjoyment and convenience** predict consistency more than any specific exercise. A lifter forced into spin classes, or a busy parent handed a 90-minute gym plan, usually quits.

This quiz looks at what you enjoy, where you like to train, how much time you have and what usually derails you. Then it matches you with one of five personalities.

## The five fitness personalities

**The Builder.** You love measurable progress: heavier weights and visible muscle. Structured strength training, 3–5 days a week, with enough protein, is your sweet spot. Try our [muscle gain program](/services/muscle-gain-program).

**The Burner.** You thrive on energy and sweat. Mixed strength and conditioning keeps you engaged. Paired with a smart calorie deficit, it's a great fit for [fat loss](/services/weight-loss-program).

**The Flow Seeker.** You care about how your body feels. Mobility, yoga and controlled strength keep you moving well for decades. See [yoga & mobility](/services/yoga-mobility).

**The Home Hero.** Time is tight, so efficiency wins. Short sessions at home, done consistently, beat a perfect plan you never follow. See [home workout plans](/services/home-workout-plans).

**The Fuel Strategist.** Food is your biggest lever. A plan built around meals you already love will move the needle faster than extra cardio. See [custom diet plans](/services/diet-plans).
