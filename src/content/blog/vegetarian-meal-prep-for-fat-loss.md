Fat loss gets much easier when good food is already waiting in your fridge. This simple vegetarian meal prep takes about 90 minutes on a weekend and covers most of your weekday meals.

## The plan at a glance

Each day uses the same structure: a high-protein breakfast, a balanced lunch box, a protein snack and a lighter dinner. It's built around everyday Indian ingredients and roughly 1,600–1,900 kcal a day, depending on portions.

## Weekend prep (about 90 minutes)

1. **Cook a big pot of dal or rajma.** Portion into 4 lunch boxes.
2. **Cook rice or millets** and portion with the dal.
3. **Make paneer or tofu bhurji** for 3 breakfasts.
4. **Roast a tray of vegetables** (capsicum, onion, cauliflower, carrots) with a teaspoon of oil.
5. **Soak and sprout moong** for snacks and salads.
6. **Hang 500 g of curd** overnight for thick, high-protein hung curd.

## A sample day

- **Breakfast:** paneer bhurji with one multigrain roti.
- **Lunch:** dal, rice and roasted vegetables.
- **Snack:** hung curd with fruit, or a sprouts chaat.
- **Dinner:** besan cheela with salad, or a vegetable khichdi with curd.

## Tips that make it work

- **Measure oil.** One tablespoon is about 120 kcal. It's the number one reason home-cooked diets stall.
- **Protein first.** Every meal should have dairy, legumes, soy or eggs.
- **Keep sauces separate** so food doesn't get soggy.
- **Prep for 3–4 days,** not 7. Food tastes better and stays safe.

## Shopping list

Moong dal, rajma or chana, paneer or tofu, curd, besan, rice or millets, multigrain atta, seasonal vegetables, onions, tomatoes, fruit, roasted chana, and your usual spices.
